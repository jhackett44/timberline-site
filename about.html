The founder

Meet Josh, founder of Timberline Fire Systems.

I spent nearly a decade working in the restaurant industry before making the move to fire suppression. Combined with over twenty years of running my own businesses, that experience shapes everything about how Timberline operates.

I've worked Friday night services. I know what it looks like when something goes wrong mid-shift, and I know exactly what an unplanned hour of downtime costs an operator. That perspective doesn't come from a fire protection textbook.

"I built the inspection company I'd want on my side if I were still working a Friday night rush."

When I made the move into fire suppression, I wasn't just learning a trade. I was building the kind of service I always wished existed on the other side of the hood.

How we work

Built around your schedule, not ours.

Timberline inspections are designed to minimize disruption. We show up on time, work efficiently, and get out of your way. You get a clear, readable report the same day.

We also believe the best client relationships are built on consistency. When you call Timberline, you get the same technician every visit. Someone who knows your system, knows your kitchen, and can spot changes before they become problems.

That's not just good service. That's how you protect an investment.

Where we work

Beaverton based. Portland metro focused.

Timberline is based in Beaverton and serves the full Portland metro including Washington, Multnomah, and Clackamas counties. Restaurants, ghost kitchens, churches, schools, food trucks, and commercial kitchens of all sizes.

Portland's food truck community is a particular focus. With over 500 food trucks operating in the metro, it's one of the most underserved markets for suppression inspection in the region.
