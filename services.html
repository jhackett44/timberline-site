What we do

Kitchen hood suppression inspection and service.

Every inspection follows NFPA 96 standards and comes with a signed report you can file with your insurer, health inspector, or fire marshal.

01
Inspection

Semi-annual inspection

Oregon law requires all fixed kitchen fire suppression systems to be inspected every six months. We perform a full system inspection per NFPA 96 and provide a signed report documenting every finding.

Most inspections are completed in under an hour. We work around your schedule to minimize disruption to your kitchen.

What's included
  • Visual inspection of all nozzles and nozzle caps
  • Fusible link inspection and replacement if needed
  • Agent cylinder, pressure gauge, and expellant cartridge inspection
  • Pull station and manual actuation device inspection
  • Hood and duct clearance check
  • Fuel and electrical interlock inspection
  • Review of system service history and tag
  • Signed inspection report documenting all findings
02
Service

System service

Beyond the inspection, suppression systems require periodic component replacement to stay operational. We handle all standard service items on the same visit when possible.

Service items are billed in addition to the inspection fee. We'll walk you through exactly what needs replacing and why before we do anything.

What's included
  • Fusible link replacement (required semi-annually)
  • Nozzle cleaning and nozzle cap replacement
  • Expellant cartridge replacement as needed
  • Agent cylinder recharge if discharged
  • Pull station cable and linkage inspection
  • System reset and operational test
03
Mobile kitchens

Food truck inspections

Food trucks operating in Portland are subject to the same suppression system requirements as fixed commercial kitchens under NFPA 96. Mobile kitchens have unique layout and access challenges that require a technician comfortable working in tight spaces.

We work directly with Portland's food truck community and understand the permitting requirements for mobile food units.

What's included
  • All standard inspection items
  • Manual actuation distance verification per NFPA 96 (10 to 20 feet for mobile applications)
  • LP gas or electric interlock connection inspection
  • Hood and cooking equipment clearance verification
  • Signed report formatted for Portland mobile food unit permitting
What we don't cover

We stay in our lane.

Timberline focuses on inspecting and servicing existing fixed fire suppression systems. A few things we don't handle:

  • Fire alarm systems
  • Fire sprinkler systems and standpipes
  • Grease duct and hood cleaning (different trade, ask us for a referral)
  • New system installation
  • Data center and server room clean agent systems, at this time

If you're not sure whether your system falls under what we do, reach out and we'll point you in the right direction, even if that's toward someone else.
